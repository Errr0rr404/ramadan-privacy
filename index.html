Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we handle your data.

Last updated: February 2026

📍 Location Data

We access your device's location solely to calculate accurate prayer times, Suhoor/Iftar times, and Qibla direction for your area. Your coordinates are sent to the Aladhan API to fetch prayer times. We do not store, log, or share your location data.

📱 Local Storage

Your preferences (language, adhan settings, app launch count) are saved locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your phone.

🔔 Notifications

The app schedules local notifications for prayer time reminders and adhan alerts. These are processed entirely on your device — no external push notification server is used.

🚫 What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • We do not store your location on any server
  • We do not share any data with third parties

🌐 Third-Party Services

The app uses the Aladhan API for prayer time calculations. This is a free, open Islamic prayer times service. No personal data beyond your approximate coordinates is sent to this service.

💳 In-App Purchases

The app offers optional tips through Apple's App Store and Google Play. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple/Google. We do not receive, store, or have access to any payment information.

📧 Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out to us through the "Write to Us" feature in the app menu, or email us at zan.sheum@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
