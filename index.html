Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 14, 2026

Ramadan 2026 ("the App") is developed and maintained by World of Z. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The App does not have user accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

2. Location Data

The App requests your device's approximate location solely to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Location data is:

  • Sent to the Aladhan prayer-times API as latitude/longitude coordinates to retrieve prayer times.
  • Processed on-device for Qibla direction calculation.
  • Never stored, logged, or shared by the App beyond the API request above.

3. Notifications

The App schedules local notifications on your device for prayer-time reminders, adhan alerts, and daily dua reminders. These notifications are entirely on-device; no push notification service or external server is involved. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or the in-app menu.

4. Audio

The App plays a bundled adhan audio file at prayer times. Dua recitation audio is streamed from Cloudinary (a content-delivery network) when you tap the Arabic text of a dua. No personal data is sent with these audio requests. No microphone access is requested or used.

5. In-App Purchases (Tip Jar)

The App offers optional tip-jar purchases processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store any payment information. All transactions are governed by the respective store's terms and privacy policies.

6. Contact / Feedback

If you choose to contact us via the "Write to Us" feature, the App opens your device's default email client. We only receive the information you voluntarily include in your email. We do not share your contact information with third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Aladhan API — receives latitude/longitude to return prayer times. See their privacy policy.
  • Cloudinary — serves dua recitation audio files. No personal data is transmitted; only the audio file URL is requested. See their privacy policy.
  • Charity links — the App links to external charity websites (Islamic Relief, LaunchGood). Visiting those sites is subject to their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). The App is suitable for all ages and contains no age-restricted content.

9. Sharing Feature

The App allows you to share a dua as an image card via the native share sheet. A temporary image is generated on-device and shared using your operating system's built-in sharing mechanism. No data is uploaded to our servers.

10. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no user data at risk. All user preferences — including language, notification settings, favorite duas, tasbeeh counts, and cached prayer times — are stored locally on your device using standard platform storage and are never transmitted externally.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
