Privacy Policy

Adhan & Iqamah

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Get the App on the App Store or on Google Play.

In short

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • For prayer times, your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to the Aladhan prayer-times service.
  • Place names and city search use your phone's own map service (Apple or Google).
  • Mosque lists and iqamah times come from public mosque directories and your mosque's timetable service, which never receive your coordinates. A mosque search can send Live Mosque the postcode or name you type or, if you search by location, your area's postcode or city.
  • Your settings, saved mosque and favorites stay on your device.

1. About this policy

Adhan & Iqamah ("the App") is developed and maintained by World of Z. This policy explains what information the App uses, where it is sent, and the choices you have.

2. What we don't collect

The App has no user accounts and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We do not run servers that store information about you, and we never sell or share personal data.

3. Location

The App uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. You can choose a city by hand instead, and then your device location is not used.

  • Prayer times: your coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are sent to the Aladhan prayer-times service (api.aladhan.com).
  • Place names: to show your city and country, and when you search for a city, the App uses your phone's built-in geocoding service — Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android. This is a network service: your coordinates, or the text you type, are sent to Apple or Google under their privacy policies. Searching for a city does not need location permission.
  • The Qibla direction and the distance to the Kaaba are calculated on your device. The compass uses your phone's sensors, which on both platforms needs location permission.
  • Your coordinates, place name and country, and any city you choose, are stored on your device so the App also works offline.

4. Mosque and iqamah times

When you choose a mosque, the App downloads that mosque's published timetable directly from the platform the mosque uses: Masjidal, Mawaqit, Prayers Connect, MadinaApps, PrayTime, My-Masjid, MasjidiApp, The Masjid App, MasjidBoard Live, Masjid-Timetable, GoPray, SalaahTimes or Live Mosque.

  • These requests contain the mosque's identifier and, like any internet request, your IP address. They do not contain your location.
  • The mosque directory is a set of public files hosted on GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com). The App downloads the list for your country, so GitHub can tell which country's list was requested.
  • When you search for a mosque, the postcode or name you type can be sent to Live Mosque's Google Firebase database to find matching mosques.
  • If you search by your location, your phone's geocoding service (see Location) turns your coordinates into a postcode or city name. Only that postcode or city name is used for the search; your coordinates are not sent to Live Mosque.
  • The mosque you select and your favorite mosques are stored only on your device.

5. Maps and directions

"Near me" and "Directions" open a map app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze) with a search for mosques or halal food around your coordinates, or with the mosque's address. What happens there is governed by that map provider's privacy policy.

6. Notifications and the adhan

Prayer reminders, the adhan and dua reminders are scheduled on your phone, and the adhan recordings are part of the App. No push-notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

7. Dua recitations

Dua recitations are downloaded from Cloudinary, a content-delivery network, the first time you play each one, and then kept on your device. These requests carry only the name of the audio file and your IP address.

8. Tips, reviews and updates

  • Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • To let you know when a new version is out, the App reads its own public store page (Apple's iTunes lookup or the Google Play listing) at most once a day.
  • "Rate the App" and "Share the App" open the store or your phone's share sheet.

9. Feedback emails

"Write to Us" opens your own email app with your message, so you can read it before you send it. To help us fix problems it adds the App version, your phone's system version, the App language, your time zone and your prayer-time calculation settings — nothing that identifies you. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use your email address only to reply to you.

10. Data on your device

  • Your settings, language, chosen location, saved mosque, favorite duas, tasbeeh counts and cached prayer and mosque times are stored on your device.
  • If your phone's backup (iCloud or Google) is on, it may include this App data in your backup, under your account.
  • Deleting the App, or clearing its data, removes all of it. We keep no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

11. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone else.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the App, and on this page, with a new effective date. Questions? Use "Write to Us" in the App menu, or email zan.sheum@gmail.com.
